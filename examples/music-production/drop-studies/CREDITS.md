# Drop studies audio credits

- The live break in Stairwell is EatMe’s [108 bpm breakbeat](https://archive.org/details/108_bpm_breakbeat_to_use), recorded with two overhead microphones and released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The library’s `played-break.wav` uses Sample00, cut at 0, 2.219, 4.444 and 6.664 seconds and repitched a bar at a time to 174 BPM. The original FLAC’s SHA-256 is `afd73dbfd96bac7a5ab424fca5a34e7b92f37d6b7bcf48f005adf90f6278dd7f`.
- The acoustic kick alternates come from [Versilian Studios’ CC0 Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/blob/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e/License.md), Bass Drum 1, level 5, takes 1 and 2, Sum microphone.
- The acoustic snare recordings are also from that library. They use `snare_f1.wav`, `snare_f2.wav` and `snare_rim.wav` from Legacy Snares/OldSnare.
